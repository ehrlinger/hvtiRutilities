The `Description` field names the main facilities (study layout and paths, data import and type conversion, labels, the study data contract with manifests, checksums and provenance, fit caching, the analysis taxonomy, and the 'SAS' procedure ports and parity checks) and spells out HVTI (Heart, Vascular and Thoracic Institute) and CORR (Cardiovascular Outcomes, Registries and Research). The `Title` is shortened to "Study and Data Utilities for the HVTI CORR Group", under the 65-character limit in Writing R Extensions. This answers the CRAN Cookbook's DESCRIPTION and General Issues chapters.
